Tile palette

All nineteen tiles, 1 through 262144, filling the real 19-cell board, snaking along the "/" diagonals so every value touches the one before and after it. Font sizing is tileFontSize() exactly as it ships.

Drag any tile onto any other and their colours trade. Numbers never move. Works within a board and across boards. Each board has its own reset, which appears once you have changed something. X is the grey workbench; A keeps today's ramp and is not draggable.

G is the palette as dragged together on X. H is the live one and the only board you can fine-tune: tap a tile for hue, saturation and lightness sliders, arrows for single steps, and a per-tile reset. Dragging still swaps on H, so a tap is a tap and a drag is a drag. J paints H's baseline — the values H resets to — so while you tune H you can still see what it looked like before.
